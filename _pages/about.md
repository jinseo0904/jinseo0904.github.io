I am a third-year PhD student in the Personal Health Informatics program at Northeastern University's [Khoury College of Computer Sciences](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/). I am a member of the [mHealth Research Group](https://www.mhealthgroup.org/), where I am advised by Professor [Stephen Intille](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/home/intille/). My work encompasses Human-Computer Interaction, Wearable/Ubiquitous Computing, and Personal Health Informatics, with a focus on **personalized content delivery and data collection.**

Before joining Northeastern, I completed my BA in Computer Science and Cognitive Science at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/) in May 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I received the [Kwanjeong Educational Foundation's](https://www.ikef.or.kr/) international student scholarship.

I am interested in...
======
- Designing innovative sensing and intervention technologies using mobile, wearable devices, and AI to:
  - **Generate and deliver personalized interactive content [(such as word puzzles!)](https://jinseo0904.github.io)** to support engagement and data validation in human-subjects data collection studies
  - Use AI to build and maintain a **comprehensive understanding of people** and use it to deliver more effective, personalized user interfaces and health interventions in real-world settings
  - Enable sustainable collection of **large-scale, intensive, longitudinal health and behavioral data, encompassing passive sensing and self-reports** for training and validating downstream AI/ML tasks including human activity recognition (HAR), just-in-time adaptive interventions (JITAIs), and more
  - Support individuals in building **and maintaining** health behaviors and habits, such as regular physical activity and healthy diet
